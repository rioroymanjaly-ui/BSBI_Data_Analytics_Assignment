# Tableau Story — 6 Points

1. **Overall performance** — Sales, Profit, Profit Ratio, orders and average order value.
2. **Growth over time** — Annual and monthly movement, including the 2015 dip and stronger later growth.
3. **Products and categories** — Which categories/sub-categories sell well and where profitability is weak.
4. **Customers and segments** — Consumer, Corporate and Home Office contribution plus Top-N customers.
5. **Geography and operating risk** — Regional/state performance, discount-profit relationship and shipping context.
6. **Management actions** — Investigate loss-making sub-categories/states, protect profitable growth and review discounting before making broad changes.
